How this works: Drag the Bias Voltage slider. Below the knee voltage, the depletion region barrier is present and current stays near zero. Past the knee voltage (0.7V for Silicon, 0.3V for Germanium), the barrier collapses and current shoots up exponentially - watch the glowing amber dot race up the curve on the I-V graph as you cross that point.
